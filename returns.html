Return Eligibility
✓

Items are eligible for return if they are unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all tags attached. Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.

The following items cannot be returned:

Worn or washed items Items without tags Sale / final-sale items Gift cards
Refund Policy

Once your return is received and inspected, we will process your refund to the original payment method. Please allow 3–5 business days for the refund to appear in your account after processing.

  • Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
  • Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility for standard returns
  • For defective or incorrect items, we cover all return shipping costs
Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges, but the process is simple: return your item for a refund, then place a new order for the size or style you'd like. This ensures you get your new item as quickly as possible.

Defective or Wrong Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the issue. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you.
