How to Measure
01
Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal under your arms.

02
Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso, usually above the belly button.

03
Inseam

Measure from the crotch to the bottom of the ankle bone along the inside of the leg.
